The idea

Productivity comes down to two things.

One

See the whole picture

Work, family and household on one timeline. See what's in motion, who owns it, and where the load piles up, from the next few weeks to a full year.

Two

Know exactly what to do, and when

A short queue for today, ordered by importance and deadline, sized to your real focus hours. Every task says why it's there.

What matters, not more.

Other apps push you to get more done. Mainthread shows you what matters and when, so everything else can wait.
